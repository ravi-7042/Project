RERA Registration

Ensure compliance with the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act (RERA) by registering your real estate project with the appropriate regulatory authority. RERA aims to promote transparency, accountability, and efficiency in the real estate sector.

Benefits of RERA registration:

  • Enhanced trust and confidence among buyers and investors.
  • Protection of buyer's rights and interests.
  • Mandatory disclosure of project details, plans, and approvals.
  • Establishment of an escrow account for project funds.
  • Timely completion of projects and adherence to promised specifications.

Documents required for RERA registration:

  • Project details, including layout plans, building plans, and approvals.
  • Developer's identity and address proof.
  • Details of past and ongoing projects.
  • Financial statements and audited accounts.
  • Other relevant documents as specified by the regulatory authority.
