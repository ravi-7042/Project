MSME Registration

Register your business under the Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act to avail benefits and incentives provided to MSMEs. MSME registration is essential to be recognized as an MSME and to access various government schemes and support.

Benefits of MSME registration:

  • Access to government schemes, subsidies, and financial assistance.
  • Priority in procurement from government entities.
  • Collateral-free loans and credit facilities.
  • Protection against delayed payments from buyers.
  • Preference in government tenders and contracts.
  • Support for technology upgrades and skill development.

Documents required for MSME registration:

  • PAN card of the business or individual.
  • Proof of business registration (such as partnership deed, certificate of incorporation, etc.).
  • Address proof of the business premises.
  • Copy of Aadhaar card of the business owner/partners/directors.
  • Bank account details.
  • Other relevant supporting documents as per the nature of the business.
