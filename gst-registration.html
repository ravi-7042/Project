GST Registration

Comply with tax laws and register your business for Goods and Services Tax (GST). GST is an indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services in India. It is mandatory for businesses with an annual turnover above a certain threshold to register for GST.

GST registration offers various benefits, such as:

  • Legally recognized as a supplier of goods or services.
  • Eligibility to collect and claim input tax credit.
  • Access to interstate sales and purchases.
  • Compliance with tax laws and avoidance of penalties.

Documents required for GST registration:

  • PAN card of the business or individual.
  • Proof of business registration (such as partnership deed, certificate of incorporation, etc.).
  • Identity and address proof of the business owner/partners/directors.
  • Address proof of the business premises.
  • Bank account details.
  • Authorization letter, if applicable.
  • Other relevant supporting documents as per the nature of the business.
