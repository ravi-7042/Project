Shop Act Registration

Comply with labor laws and obtain the necessary licenses by registering your shop or establishment under the Shop and Establishment Act. Shop Act registration is mandatory for businesses operating within a specified jurisdiction.

Benefits of Shop Act registration:

  • Legal recognition and compliance with labor laws.
  • Eligibility to obtain other required licenses and permits.
  • Establishment as a legitimate business entity.
  • Employee welfare and protection.
  • Smooth business operations and hassle-free inspections.

Documents required for Shop Act registration:

  • PAN card of the business or individual.
  • Proof of business registration (such as partnership deed, certificate of incorporation, etc.).
  • Identity and address proof of the business owner/partners/directors.
  • Address proof of the business premises.
  • Employee details, such as their names and addresses.
  • Other relevant supporting documents as per the nature of the business.
