FCRA Registration

Comply with the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act (FCRA) by obtaining FCRA registration or prior permission. FCRA regulates the acceptance and utilization of foreign contribution or donation by individuals, associations, and organizations in India.

Benefits of FCRA registration:

  • Eligibility to receive foreign contributions legally.
  • Enhanced credibility and trust among donors and stakeholders.
  • Opportunity to contribute to social welfare activities and causes.
  • Compliance with legal requirements and avoidance of penalties.

Documents required for FCRA registration:

  • Proof of registration of the organization.
  • Memorandum of Association (MOA) and Articles of Association (AOA).
  • Bank account details and financial statements.
  • Previous three years' audited accounts, if applicable.
  • Activity report and audited statements of the past three years, if applicable.
  • Details of key office bearers and members.
  • Other relevant documents as specified by the regulatory authority.
