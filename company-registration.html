Company Registration

Register your company and establish a legal entity for your business. Company registration provides various advantages and legal protections.

Benefits of company registration:

  • Limited liability protection for the company's shareholders.
  • Separate legal entity from the owners.
  • Ability to raise funds through the issuance of shares.
  • Enhanced credibility and trust among customers and partners.
  • Access to government schemes and incentives.

Documents required for company registration:

  • Identity and address proof of directors and shareholders.
  • PAN card of the directors and shareholders.
  • Proof of registered office address.
  • Memorandum of Association (MOA) and Articles of Association (AOA).
  • DIN (Director Identification Number) for the directors.
  • Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for online filing.
